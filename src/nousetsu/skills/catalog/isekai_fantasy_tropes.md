### Isekai & Adventurer Guild Conventions:
- Standardize Adventurer Guild ranks (F-Rank through S-Rank / Mythic) and quest difficulty tiers.
- Format magical chanting or incantations in italics with measured, arcane rhythm.
- Treat reincarnation / transmigration jargon naturally (e.g., "otherworlder", "transmigrator", "cheat skill") without over-explaining.
